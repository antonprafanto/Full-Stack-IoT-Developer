# Sources & Image Credits — Module 21

A recap of all the images in this folder. The original curriculum diagrams are licensed **CC BY 4.0**, and their `.svg` files are included so they can be edited. The numbers, messages, and examples in the diagrams come from the author's lab for this module: backend v1.0 and a mock broker on a Windows laptop with the lab database, plus Node 1 firmware v1.6.0 tested on Wokwi. **Mosquitto isn't installed on the author's laptop this week**, so the broker behavior and log text in the diagrams are written from the official Mosquitto 2.0.21 (Pi) and 2.1.2 (laptop) source; the client-library reactions were tested through a mock broker. The example keys and fingerprints in the pictures were made just for the examples: not one of them belongs to a real device or account. All sources accessed October 2026.
